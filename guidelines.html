Technical Specifications

Manuscript Formatting Requirements

Parameter Specification
File Format Microsoft Word format (.doc or .docx) only.
Word Count 6,000 to 8,000 words (inclusive of references, tables, and appendices).
Typography Times New Roman, 12 pt font throughout.
Line Spacing 1.5 line spacing on A4 page setup with standard 1-inch (2.54 cm) margins.
Title & Running Head Title concise (< 20 words); Running head < 40 characters in header.
Abstract & Keywords Structured abstract of 150–250 words + 3–6 keywords and JEL codes.
Referencing Style Strictly APA 7th Edition with mandatory DOIs where available.
Plagiarism Policy

All submissions are checked using Turnitin before review.

Overall Similarity: Strictly < 15%
Single Source Limit: Max 3%
Publication Fee

To sustain open access and digital preservation:

Fee: ₦40,000
Payable: Only upon formal acceptance
Submission Fee: Free
Standard Structure

Manuscript Organization (IMRAD)

1. Title Page (Separate): Author names, institutional affiliations, ORCID iD, and corresponding author email.
2. Blinded Manuscript: Title, Abstract, Keywords, and JEL codes without any identifying author info.
3. Introduction & Problem: Background, clear research question, theoretical framing, and policy relevance.
4. Literature Review: Empirical review, conceptual framework, and underlying economic hypotheses.
5. Methodology: Econometric specifications, data sources, sample sizes, and diagnostic tests.
6. Results & Policy Implications: Empirical findings, policy recommendations, and conclusion.
Submission Checklist
  • MS Word (.doc/.docx) format
  • Length: 6,000 – 8,000 words
  • Times New Roman 12pt, 1.5 spaced
  • Abstract (150–250 words) + JEL codes
  • APA 7th referencing with DOIs
  • Turnitin similarity strictly < 15%
  • Blinded manuscript for peer review
