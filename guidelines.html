Section 1 & 2

Categories of Manuscripts Accepted

The African Journal of Finance, Taxation, and Fiscal Policy (AJFTFP) is a peer-reviewed academic journal published quarterly (January, April, July, October) by the Department of Management Sciences, Federal University Dutse.

The journal accepts high-quality submissions in the following categories:

1. Original Research Articles

Empirical, theoretical, or econometric studies providing novel contributions to finance, taxation, or fiscal governance (6,000–8,000 words).

2. Review Articles

Systematic literature reviews, meta-analyses, and conceptual syntheses evaluating critical fiscal frameworks.

3. Policy Analysis Papers

Rigorous assessments of ongoing or proposed fiscal reforms, budget frameworks, and tax legislation in Africa.

4. Case Studies & Book Reviews

In-depth sub-national fiscal case studies, institutional reviews, and scholarly critiques of recent books.

Section 3 & Plagiarism Guide

Originality & Plagiarism Policy

Strict Policy: Zero Tolerance on Plagiarism
All manuscripts undergo automated AI detection and plagiarism screening through Turnitin prior to editorial review.
  • Maximum Similarity Index: Must be strictly less than 15% overall.
  • Single-Source Matches: Must not exceed 3% for any individual source, based on international best practice.
  • Originality Declaration: The manuscript must be original work of the author(s), not previously published, and not under consideration by another journal. A signed declaration must accompany all submissions.
Section 4

Manuscript Formatting Requirements

Font & Size Times New Roman, 12 pt font size throughout the document.
Line Spacing & Margins 1.5 line spacing (or double spaced) with 1-inch (2.54 cm / 30 mm) margins on all sides. Tables single spaced.
Word & Page Limit 6,000 – 8,000 words (maximum 18–20 galley-proof pages including figures, tables, and references). Maximum 25 pages permitted at extra cost.
Page Formatting Standard A4 page format. All pages must be numbered consecutively at bottom centre, starting with title page.
File Format Microsoft Word (.doc or .docx) supplied as a single file containing all tables, figures, and text.
Referencing Style APA 7th Edition of Referencing Style (American Psychological Association).
Section 5 & 6

Standard Manuscript Structure

Manuscripts must be organized in the following sequential order:

1
Separate Title Page (Cover Sheet)

Contains concise, informative title (no abbreviations, avoid lengthy titles), author full names, institutional affiliations, postal address, telephone, email of corresponding author, and a short running title (< 40 characters).

2
Abstract & Keywords (Fresh Page)

150–250 words on a new page without technical econometrics. Must clearly state: (a) Research Problem, (b) Methodology, (c) Key Findings, (d) Contribution to Knowledge. Followed by 3 to 6 keywords in alphabetical order, JEL classification codes, and paper classification category.

3
Introduction & Literature Review

Conveys background, motivation, research gap, objectives, and robust theoretical & empirical review.

4
Methodology, Results & Discussion

Details econometric/statistical design, sampling, equations, empirical findings, robustness tests, and analytical discussion.

5
Conclusion, Policy Implications & Acknowledgments

Summarizes core takeaways, actionable fiscal policy recommendations, research limitations, and full disclosure of research grants, commercial links, and institutional support.

6
References (APA 7th Edition)

Alphabetically arranged complete citations with active DOIs where available.

Section 7

Tables, Figures, Equations & Style

  • Tables: Numbered consecutively as “Table 1”, “Table 2”. Short self-explanatory titles above each table and accompanying source explanation and footnotes below. Avoid referencing "table above" or "table below". Single line spacing.
  • Illustrations & Figures: Clearly labelled as “Fig. 1”, “Fig. 2” with legends. Submitted in high resolution suitable for reproduction. Photographs must be glossy quality.
  • Heading Hierarchy: Strict sequence: 1, 1.1, 1.2, 1.1.1, 1.1.2. Refrain from level section numbering.
  • Mathematics & Symbols: Properly typewritten with all Greek and special symbols identified. Vectors in boldface (indicated by wavy underline in drafts). Equations numbered serially on right-hand side in parentheses: (1).
  • Footnotes: In-text footnotes are not permitted; integrate directly into narrative text.
