Call for Submissions: Volume 1, Issue 2

The Editorial Board of the African Journal of Finance, Taxation, and Fiscal Policy (AJFTFP) invites academic staff, researchers, postgraduate scholars, and policy practitioners to submit original, unpublished manuscripts for consideration in the upcoming April 2026 issue.

Submissions Scope

Principal Thematic Focus Areas

We particularly encourage empirical and policy-focused submissions addressing the following subject areas:

1. Digital Economy & Tax Technology

E-commerce taxation, crypto-assets regulation, automated e-invoicing, and AI in fraud detection.

2. Non-Oil Revenue Mobilization

Expanding the tax base, taxing high-net-worth individuals, and formalizing informal African sectors.

3. Public Debt & Fiscal Federalism

Sovereign debt sustainability, sub-national debt ceilings, and horizontal/vertical revenue sharing formulas.

4. Environmental & Green Taxes

Carbon pricing, energy transition levies, extractive resource taxation, and green budget frameworks.

5. International Tax & BEPS

OECD Pillar 1 & 2 implementation, transfer pricing disputes, and double taxation treaty renegotiations.

6. Public Sector Financial Governance

IPSAS implementation, Treasury Single Account (TSA) reforms, and anti-corruption audit mechanisms.
