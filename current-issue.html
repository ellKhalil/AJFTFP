Coverage Areas

Themes Accepted for the Maiden Edition

Authors and researchers are invited to submit papers addressing critical theoretical, empirical, and policy questions in the following subject tracks:

Tax Policy & Administration

Direct and indirect taxes, e-taxation, tax compliance, taxpayer psychology, transfer pricing, and revenue mobilization in developing economies.

Fiscal Federalism & Public Debt

Intergovernmental fiscal relations, sub-national debt sustainability, revenue sharing formulas, public financial management, and budgeting reforms.

Corporate Finance & Governance

Capital structure, corporate financial reporting, earnings management, auditing quality, and financial sector stability in Africa.

Environmental & Digital Taxation

Carbon taxation, green fiscal incentives, artificial intelligence in tax auditing, and taxation of cross-border digital transactions.

Author Checklist

Submission Requirements

  • Word Limit: 6,000 – 8,000 words
  • Abstract: Max 250 words + 4–6 keywords
  • Referencing Style: APA 7th Edition
  • Plagiarism Limit: Turnitin < 15% similarity
  • Review Process: Double-blind (1–4 weeks)
  • Publication Fee: ₦40,000 upon acceptance
