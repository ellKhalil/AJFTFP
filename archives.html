Academic Scope

Indexed Subject Disciplines

When published, all articles will be fully indexed under the following subject tracks with permanent Crossref DOIs:

Taxation & Administration

Tax compliance, digital taxation, transfer pricing, indirect taxation, and revenue authority systems.

Fiscal Policy & Public Finance

Fiscal federalism, sub-national debt, public expenditure, and resource allocation models.

Corporate Finance & Auditing

Financial reporting, corporate governance, capital markets, and financial management.

Environmental & International Tax

Carbon pricing, green fiscal policy, cross-border tax incentives, and trade protocols.
