Policies & Publication Ethics

DOAJ Compliance Standards, Creative Commons Licensing, Copyright Retention & COPE Ethical Guidelines

DOAJ Mandatory Policy 1

Open Access Statement

Gold Open Access Declaration

The African Journal of Finance, Taxation, and Fiscal Policy (AJFTFP) provides immediate, permanent, and barrier-free open access to all its published content on the principle that making research freely available to the public supports a greater global exchange of knowledge.

In accordance with the Budapest Open Access Initiative (BOAI) definition of Open Access, users have the right to read, download, copy, distribute, print, search, or link to the full texts of all articles, crawl them for indexing, pass them as data to software, or use them for any other lawful purpose, without financial, legal, or technical barriers other than those inseparable from gaining access to the internet itself.

DOAJ Mandatory Policy 2 & 3

Copyright & Licensing Terms

1. Author Retains Copyright

Authors contributing to AJFTFP retain the copyright to their scholarly work without restriction. Authors grant the journal a non-exclusive license to publish, communicate, index, and archive the article in all formats and media.

2. Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)

All articles published in AJFTFP are licensed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0).

Under this license, anyone is free to:

  • Share — copy and redistribute the material in any medium or format.
  • Adapt — remix, transform, and build upon the material for any purpose, even commercially.

Under the following terms: You must give appropriate credit to the original author(s) and source, provide a link to the license, and indicate if changes were made.

DOAJ Mandatory Policy 4

Digital Archiving & Preservation Policy

To ensure the long-term digital preservation and permanent accessibility of all scholarly articles, AJFTFP utilizes distributed digital preservation systems:

PKP Preservation Network & LOCKSS

The journal participates in the PKP Preservation Network (PKP PN) and supports LOCKSS/CLOCKSS archival protocols, ensuring decentralized, permanent archiving across participating libraries.

Institutional Repository (FUD)

All issues and article galleys are deposited permanently in the Federal University Dutse Institutional Digital Repository and backed up with off-site cloud mirrors.

COPE Standards

Publication Ethics & Malpractice Statement

AJFTFP adheres to the ethical guidelines and Code of Conduct established by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE). We maintain the highest standards of integrity for all parties involved: Authors, Reviewers, Editors, and the Publisher.

  • Reporting Standards: Authors must present accurate accounts of original research and an objective discussion of its significance. Underlying data must be represented accurately.
  • Plagiarism & Originality: Zero tolerance for plagiarism. Turnitin similarity must be < 15%, and single source matches < 3%. Plagiarized submissions will be rejected immediately.
  • Multiple / Redundant Publication: Submitting the same manuscript to more than one journal concurrently constitutes unethical publishing behavior.
  • Authorship Attribution: Authorship must be limited to those who have made significant intellectual contributions to conception, design, execution, or interpretation.
  • Disclosure & Conflicts of Interest: Authors must disclose all financial grants, commercial links, or personal relationships that could influence the work.

  • Contribution to Editorial Decisions: Peer review assists the Editor-in-Chief in making editorial decisions and helps authors improve their papers.
  • Confidentiality: Any manuscripts received for review must be treated as confidential documents.
  • Standards of Objectivity: Reviews should be conducted objectively. Personal criticism of authors is inappropriate.
  • Promptness: Reviewers who feel unqualified or cannot meet the 2–3 weeks review window must notify the editors promptly.

  • Publication Decisions: The Editor-in-Chief evaluates manuscripts for intellectual content without regard to race, gender, religious belief, or citizenship of authors.
  • Confidentiality: Editors must not disclose any information about a submitted manuscript to anyone other than the corresponding author, reviewers, and publisher.
  • Editorial Independence: Editorial decisions are made strictly on academic merit, independent of commercial or institutional pressures.

In cases of proven scientific misconduct, fraudulent data, severe plagiarism, or honest errors that undermine findings, AJFTFP adheres to COPE Retraction Guidelines:

  • Corrigenda / Errata: Published if small errors or minor omissions occur without compromising findings.
  • Expressions of Concern: Issued by the Editor-in-Chief if an ongoing investigation is underway regarding research integrity.
  • Retractions: Published with a clear retraction note detailing reasons and consequences, linked directly to the original article DOI.
