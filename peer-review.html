Peer Review Policy & Workflow

Rigorous, Transparent, Double-Blind Peer Review System (1–4 Weeks Timeline)

Academic Quality Control

Double-Blind Peer Review System

The African Journal of Finance, Taxation, and Fiscal Policy (AJFTFP) adopts a strict double-blind peer review system to guarantee academic integrity, objectivity, fairness, and scientific excellence.

Under this system, the identity of the authors is concealed from the reviewers, and the identity of the reviewers is concealed from the authors throughout the evaluation lifecycle. All submissions are judged strictly on their merit, methodological robustness, originality, and significance to the fields of finance, taxation, and fiscal governance.

Step-by-Step Process

The 4-Stage Review Workflow

1
Editorial Screening (Desk Review)
Days 1–5

The Managing Editor and Assistant Editor-in-Chief perform preliminary screening of the manuscript to verify:

  • Alignment with the journal’s aims and scope.
  • Compliance with formatting guidelines (Times New Roman 12pt, APA 7th, 6k–8k words).
  • Automated plagiarism screening using Turnitin (< 15% similarity index, max 3% single source).
2
Reviewer Assignment
Days 5–7

Manuscripts passing desk review are assigned to a relevant Associate Editor and sent to at least two (2) independent subject-matter expert reviewers in the relevant discipline.

3
Double-Blind Peer Evaluation
Weeks 1–3

Reviewers evaluate the blinded manuscript based on the journal’s rigorous evaluation criteria:

  • Theoretical foundation, clarity of research problem, and conceptual framework.
  • Methodological rigor, sampling design, econometric modeling, and empirical validity.
  • Novel contribution to African and global fiscal policy discourse.
4
Editorial Decision & Communication
Weeks 1–4

The Editor-in-Chief synthesizes reviewer reports and conveys the formal editorial decision to the corresponding author, accompanied by detailed constructive feedback.

Outcome Categories

Possible Editorial Decisions

a. Accept

The manuscript meets all scientific and stylistic standards and is accepted for production without further alterations.

b. Accept with Minor Revisions

Authors are requested to address minor textual clarifications, referencing updates, or table formatting within 7–10 days.

c. Revise and Resubmit

The manuscript requires substantial theoretical, data, or econometric revisions. A second round of peer review will be conducted upon resubmission.

d. Reject

The manuscript falls outside the journal's scope, exhibits fatal methodological flaws, or fails originality requirements.

COPE Standards

Reviewer Guidelines & Ethical Conduct

Peer reviewers must adhere strictly to Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) guidelines:

  • Confidentiality: Treat all received manuscripts as privileged and confidential documents. Reviewers must not share or discuss unpublished papers.
  • Conflict of Interest: Immediately decline review invitations where any financial, personal, or institutional conflict of interest exists.
  • Objectivity: Reviews should be conducted objectively. Constructive, respectful, and scholarly feedback is required.
  • Timeliness: Commit to providing written review reports within the stipulated 2–3 weeks timeframe.
Review Metrics
  • Review Model: Double-Blind
  • Reviewers per Paper: Minimum 2 Experts
  • Desk Review: 3 – 5 Days
  • Average Decision Time: 1 – 4 Weeks
