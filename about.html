About AJFTFP

Vision, Mission, Aims & Scope, Governance, and Department of Management Sciences Affiliation

Journal Background & Rationale

Overview of the Journal

Academic journals play a critical role in the dissemination of knowledge, advancement of research, and development of scholarly communities. Universities across the world establish academic journals to promote intellectual discourse and enhance institutional research visibility. In recognition of this role, the Department of Management Sciences, Federal University Dutse (FUD), Jigawa State, Nigeria established the African Journal of Finance, Taxation, and Fiscal Policy (AJFTFP).

The increasing complexity of fiscal governance, tax reforms, and financial regulation across Africa has generated significant academic interest in these fields. However, there remains a limited number of specialized academic journals focusing specifically on finance, taxation, and fiscal policy within the African context. AJFTFP provides a premier scholarly platform for empirical and theoretical research addressing these critical issues while strengthening the research profile of Federal University Dutse.

Vision Statement

To be a leading African journal advancing cutting-edge research, intellectual scholarship, and policy dialogue in finance, taxation, and fiscal policy.

Mission Statement

To publish high-quality peer-reviewed research that promotes evidence-based fiscal policy, strengthens tax systems, and enhances financial governance in Africa and globally.

Aim & Objectives

Primary Aim: To promote high-quality scholarly research and policy dialogue in finance, taxation, and fiscal policy.

Key Objectives:

  • Original Research: Publish innovative, high-impact research in finance, taxation, and fiscal policy.
  • Interdisciplinary Focus: Encourage interdisciplinary research connecting fiscal governance, economics, law, and corporate finance.
  • Evidence-Based Discourse: Promote empirical and policy-driven discussions addressing tax gaps, illicit financial flows, and public debt.
  • Scholar-Policymaker Synergy: Facilitate active collaboration and dialogue between academic researchers, revenue authorities, and policymakers.
  • Global Research Visibility: Enhance the international research visibility and academic standing of Federal University Dutse.
Disciplinary Coverage

Scope of the Journal

The journal welcomes original research papers, review articles, policy analysis papers, case studies, and book reviews across the entire realm of Management Sciences, Social Sciences, Law, and Economics. Principal subject areas include:

  • Taxation & Tax Administration: Direct/indirect taxation, tax compliance, electronic tax systems, tax evasion, and tax audit.
  • Fiscal Policy & Public Finance: Fiscal sustainability, public expenditure, government budgets, and sovereign debt.
  • Corporate Finance & Governance: Capital structure, financial reporting, auditing standards, asset pricing, and business analysis.
  • International Taxation: Base erosion and profit shifting (BEPS), transfer pricing, double taxation treaties, and global tax reforms.
  • Tax Law and Policy: Legislative analysis, constitutional tax provisions, taxpayer rights, and regulatory frameworks.
  • Fiscal Federalism: Intergovernmental fiscal relations, horizontal/vertical revenue sharing, and subnational debt.
  • Environmental Taxation: Carbon taxes, green fiscal reforms, resource taxation, and petroleum profit tax.
  • Digital Economy Taxation: E-commerce taxation, crypto-assets, fintech regulation, and digital services tax (DST).
  • Public Sector Financial Management: IPSAS compliance, Treasury Single Account (TSA), and public procurement.
  • Revenue Mobilization Strategies: Non-oil revenue generation, tax technology, informal sector taxation, and IGR enhancement.
Target Contributors

AJFTFP accepts submissions from academic staff of universities, independent researchers and scholars, postgraduate students, policy experts and fiscal practitioners, and international scholars in related fields.

Visual Identity

Symbolism of the Journal Logo & Cover

The logo and visual identity of the African Journal of Finance, Taxation and Fiscal Policy visually communicate the journal’s academic identity, thematic focus, and African geographical orientation:

Circular Structure

Represents academic unity, collaboration, continuity in scholarly research, and institutional credibility & authority.

Golden Map of Africa

Signifies geographical focus, promotion of African scholarship, economic potential, natural resource wealth, and prosperity.

Arrow & Bar Chart

Symbolizes economic growth, fiscal expansion, revenue generation, and quantitative empirical/statistical analysis.

International Currency Symbols

₦ (Naira), $ (Dollar), € (Euro), £ (Pound), and K (Kwacha) representing domestic African currencies and global capital markets.

Coin Stacks

Represent government revenue, tax collection, public finance management, and fiscal policy implementation.

Radiating Background Pattern

Symbolizes knowledge dissemination, academic enlightenment, and the societal impact of research.

Official Colour Palette

Navy Blue: Symbolizes academic excellence, authority, professionalism, and institutional credibility.
Gold: Represents prestige, quality, economic prosperity, and intellectual value.
