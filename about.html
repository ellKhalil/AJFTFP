About the Journal

Vision, Mission, Aims & Scope, Institutional Affiliation, Logo Symbolism, and Indexing

Journal Background

Overview of the Journal

The African Journal of Finance, Taxation, and Fiscal Policy (AJFTFP) is a quarterly, peer-reviewed, open-access academic journal published in January, April, July, and October by the Department of Management Sciences, Federal University Dutse (FUD), Jigawa State, Nigeria.

The journal provides an intellectual bridge between cutting-edge theoretical modeling, empirical econometric investigations, and pragmatic policy formulation. It serves as an authoritative platform for scholars, tax administrators, central bankers, fiscal analysts, and development practitioners across Africa and the global academic community.

Our Vision

To be a premier African journal championing transformational research, intellectual discourse, and evidence-based policy formulation in finance, taxation, and fiscal governance.

Our Mission

To advance rigorous, policy-relevant scholarship that strengthens African tax systems, optimizes revenue mobilization, promotes transparent fiscal federalism, and accelerates sustainable economic growth.

Key Journal Objectives
1. Rigorous Scholarly Platform: Publish high-impact research on African financial systems and fiscal governance.
2. Evidence-Based Policy: Bridge empirical econometric analysis with public sector tax administration.
3. Emerging Issues: Address digital taxation, informal sector formalization, and sovereign debt.
4. Global Dialogue: Foster interdisciplinary discourse among researchers, policymakers, and institutions.

Coverage & Disciplines

Aims and Scope

AJFTFP publishes original empirical research, theoretical developments, review articles, policy analyses, and case studies. Our core coverage spans four interdisciplinary pillars:

1. Taxation & Tax Administration
  • Direct & Indirect taxation, VAT, and Custom Duties
  • Digital economy taxation & cross-border transactions
  • Tax compliance behavior, evasion, and avoidance
  • Tax administration technology, e-filing, and automation
  • Informal sector tax mobilization & formalization
2. Fiscal Policy & Public Finance
  • Fiscal federalism & intergovernmental revenue allocation
  • Public expenditure management & budgetary efficiency
  • Sovereign debt management & fiscal sustainability
  • Medium-Term Expenditure Frameworks (MTEF)
  • Fiscal transparency, accountability, and rule-of-law
3. Corporate Finance & Accounting
  • Corporate financial reporting & IFRS compliance
  • Auditing standards, forensic accounting, and fraud control
  • Capital structure, dividend policy, and valuation
  • Corporate governance and board composition
  • Financial technologies (FinTech) & banking systems
4. International & Emerging Issues
  • International tax treaties & Base Erosion and Profit Shifting (BEPS)
  • Transfer pricing regulations in emerging markets
  • Environmental taxation & green fiscal reform
  • African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) fiscal impacts
  • Extractive industry fiscal regimes and resource revenue
Visual Identity

Official Seal & Logo Symbolism

The emblem of the African Journal of Finance, Taxation, and Fiscal Policy was thoughtfully designed to encapsulate the multidisciplinary identity, geographical scope, and academic excellence of the journal.

African Map Contour:

Represents the continental reach and pan-African focus on indigenous fiscal realities.

Currency Symbols:

Reflect monetary economics, foreign exchange dynamics, and international finance.

Ascending Bar Chart:

Signifies empirical econometric modeling, continuous growth, and revenue yield optimization.

Stacked Coins:

Represents capital accumulation, treasury management, and fiscal resource mobilization.

Navy Blue & Gold Palette:

Deep navy blue represents institutional integrity and academic depth; warm gold signifies prosperity and scholarly excellence.
