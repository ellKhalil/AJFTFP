Governance Structure

Editorial Responsibilities Framework

This Editorial Responsibilities Framework outlines the governance structure, roles, and responsibilities of members of the Editorial Team of the African Journal of Finance, Taxation, and Fiscal Policy (AJFTFP). The framework is designed to ensure that the journal operates according to international best practices in scholarly publishing, maintains high academic and ethical standards, and complies with requirements for journal registration, indexing, and accreditation by relevant bodies.

The Editor-in-Chief is the chief academic and administrative officer of the journal and is responsible for overall editorial policy, academic integrity, and strategic direction:

  • Provide overall leadership and strategic direction for the journal.
  • Establish and enforce editorial policies, standards, and ethical guidelines.
  • Oversee the peer review process and ensure fairness, transparency, and academic integrity.
  • Make final decisions on the acceptance or rejection of submitted manuscripts.
  • Appoint members of the editorial board in consultation with the journal committee or department.
  • Represent the journal in academic and professional engagements locally and internationally.
  • Ensure compliance with international publishing standards and indexing requirements (DOAJ, Crossref, AJOL).
  • Prepare annual editorial reports on journal performance and development.

  • Assist the Editor-in-Chief in managing the editorial workflow.
  • Conduct preliminary screening of manuscripts for relevance and compliance with submission guidelines.
  • Coordinate the peer review process by assigning manuscripts to reviewers or Associate Editors.
  • Monitor review timelines to ensure timely editorial decisions (1–4 weeks).
  • Provide recommendations to the Editor-in-Chief regarding editorial decisions.
  • Perform the duties of the Editor-in-Chief when delegated.

Responsible for administrative, operational, and editorial coordination of the journal:

  • Editorial Administration: Coordinate day-to-day operations and maintain records of board meetings and correspondences.
  • Manuscript Management: Receive and acknowledge submissions; conduct initial screening for formatting and Turnitin plagiarism limits; track progress throughout review.
  • Coordination of Peer Review: Manage double-blind peer review; maintain reviewer confidentiality; follow up on review reports.
  • Production & Publication: Coordinate copyediting, proofreading, layout; assign DOIs; prepare manuscripts for online publication.
  • Quality Assurance & Ethics: Screen all submissions through Turnitin (<15% threshold); enforce COPE ethical compliance.

Technical Editor: Responsible for technical quality, APA 7th style compliance, copyediting, final galley layouts, metadata tagging for Google Scholar and indexing databases, and digital archiving.

Associate Editors: Manage assigned manuscripts in their specialized domain; evaluate reviewer comments; make objective recommendations to the Editor-in-Chief.

Business Editor: Oversees financial sustainability, individual/institutional subscriptions (2026 rates), marketing, sponsorship, and financial reporting.

Journal Secretary: Coordinates administrative records, meeting agendas, author correspondences, and documentation required for journal registration and ISSN accreditation.

Ethical & Professional Standards

All members of the editorial team adhere to internationally recognized standards of academic publishing, including complete editorial independence, manuscript confidentiality, strict avoidance of conflicts of interest, transparent peer review, and zero tolerance for plagiarism.
