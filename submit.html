Step 1: Corresponding Author Information

Step 2: Manuscript Metadata

State: Research problem, methodology, key findings, and contribution to knowledge.

Step 3: Document Uploads

Must contain NO author identifiers for double-blind review.
Contains title, full names, affiliations, addresses, emails, and phones.
Confirming work has not been published or submitted elsewhere.
Raw data tables, questionnaires, or high-res figures.

Email Submission Option

Authors who experience technical issues with the online portal may submit directly via email to the Editorial Office.

Primary Email: ajftfp@fud.edu.ng
Alternate: editor@fud.edu.ng

Please attach: (1) Blinded Manuscript, (2) Title Cover Sheet, and (3) Signed Originality Covering Letter.

Submission Checklist
  • MS Word document format (.doc/.docx)
  • Times New Roman 12pt, 1.5 line spacing
  • Word limit 6,000–8,000 words
  • 150–250 words abstract + 3–6 keywords
  • APA 7th Edition referencing
  • Blinded file (no author names in text)
  • Separate Title page with full affiliations
  • Signed covering letter / declaration
